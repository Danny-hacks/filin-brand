Privacy Policy

At Engen Petroleum (Mauritius) Limited ("the Company", "we", "our", "us"), we are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Data Protection Act 2017 of Mauritius and applicable international data protection standards.

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data Controller

Engen Petroleum (Mauritius) Limited is the data controller responsible for the processing of your personal data collected through this website.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

  • Identification data (name, surname)
  • Contact details (email address, phone number)
  • Business information (company name, position)
  • Technical data (IP address, browser type, device information)
  • Any information voluntarily submitted via forms or communications

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on:

  • Your consent
  • The performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures
  • Compliance with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Our legitimate business interests, where such interests do not override your rights

4. Purpose of Processing

Your personal data is used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests
  • Provide services and manage business relationships
  • Improve website performance and user experience
  • Ensure compliance with legal obligations

5. Data Sharing and Transfers

We may share your personal data with:

  • Authorised service providers and partners
  • Regulatory authorities where required by law

Where data is transferred outside Mauritius, we ensure appropriate safeguards in line with the Data Protection Act and internationally recognised standards.

6. Data Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law.

7. Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act 2017, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Office of Mauritius

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

9. Contact

For any data protection queries, please contact our team via the Contact page or write to us at:
