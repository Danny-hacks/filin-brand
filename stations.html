    At Every Station

    More Than a Stop -
    A Destination

    Every Fillin station is built around the customer. From quality fuel to hot food and premium retail - here's what you'll find across our 37-station network.

    Fuels

    Premium Mogas and Gasoil at every site, sourced and quality-tested to meet Mauritius Energy Services Company specifications - reliable performance for every vehicle.

    ShopIn

    A well-curated convenience store stocked with snacks, beverages, household essentials, and travel necessities - everything you need, right at the forecourt.

    SipIn

    A refreshing range of beverages - from chilled drinks and juices to hot beverages - available for grab-and-go or enjoyed at the station.

    EatIn

    Hot meals, quick bites, and fresh food options - prepared on-site and perfect for a stop during your commute or a longer break on a road trip.

    Fleet & Commercial

    Engen 1Card fleet payment solutions and commercial bulk fuel supply - tailored for businesses operating across all industries in Mauritius.

    Open 24/7

    Eight stations across the network operate around the clock - so you always have access to fuel, convenience retail, and essential services, day or night.
