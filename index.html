Services Overview

Built around the way the day moves

From the first fill before work to a quick coffee, a top-up on essentials or lubricant advice for your vehicle — Fillin brings everything into one island-wide network. Consistent service, familiar stops, no detours.

  • Mogas Petrol for morning commutes, school runs, family errands and island travel.
  • Gasoil Diesel for tradespeople, delivery routes, fleet movement and heavier daily work.
  • Lubricants Automotive and industrial oils that help vehicles and equipment keep going.
  • QuickShop Everyday essentials, snacks and drinks for quick stops between destinations.
  • Cafe 365 Coffee, food and refreshments for early starts, lunch breaks and late journeys.

    Sustainability & Safety

    Care in every
    Fillin stop.

    Behind every visit is a simple promise: safer forecourts, attentive teams and responsible operations that respect the people who move Mauritius.

    From daily safety checks to the HSEQ standards that guide every team member, Fillin is built to feel reliable, human and locally aware — a brand that earns its place on the island by getting the basics right, every time.

    • Safer visits Clear HSEQ practices help protect customers, staff and station partners. From fire safety protocols to spill response, every team follows documented standards at every stop.
    • People-first service Trained attendants, well-maintained forecourts and a culture built around getting it right for the customer — every visit, every station.
    • Made for Mauritius Island-aware planning, local hiring and station placement that puts service where people in Mauritius actually go — every day.
