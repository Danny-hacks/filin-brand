Accessibility Statement

At Engen Petroleum (Mauritius) Limited, we are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, in line with international accessibility standards and best practices.

Last updated: May 2026

1. Our Commitment

We aim to make our website accessible, inclusive, and user-friendly for all individuals, including persons with disabilities. We believe that everyone deserves equal access to information and services online, and we are committed to removing barriers that may prevent this.

2. Standards

We strive to align with recognised standards such as:

  • Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) - we aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance
  • International best practices for digital accessibility
  • Applicable Mauritian regulations and standards regarding digital inclusion

Our accessibility features include semantic HTML markup, keyboard navigability, ARIA labels and roles, sufficient colour contrast, and alt text on all meaningful images.

3. Ongoing Improvements

We continuously monitor and improve our website to enhance accessibility and usability. Our development team regularly reviews the site against accessibility guidelines and conducts testing to identify and address any barriers that may exist. We treat accessibility as an ongoing commitment rather than a one-time task.

4. Feedback

If you encounter any accessibility barriers or require assistance accessing content on our website, please contact us. Your feedback is important and helps us improve the experience for all users. We aim to respond to accessibility queries within 5 business days.

Please reach out via our Contact page or call us on +230 206 6000.

5. Alternative Access

We will make reasonable efforts to provide information in alternative formats upon request. If you are unable to access any content or functionality on our website due to a disability, please contact us and we will work with you to provide the information or service you need through an alternative means.
