Why Work With Us

More Than a Job - A Purpose

At Engen Petroleum (Mauritius) Limited, working with us means being part of something bigger than a job. It means contributing to a brand built for Mauritius, by Mauritians - where when you stop to refill your car, you also refill yourself.

We are not just a fuel company. We are a brand that believes in care, dignity, and human connection. Our purpose is simple: to serve people, support their journeys, and create moments that go beyond the transaction.

"Lesans nou pei" - a business that truly belongs to Mauritius.

Our Culture

Three Pillars of How We Work

Our way of working is guided by three core pillars that define who we are and how we show up - for our customers, our communities, and each other.

01
Care

We look after our people, our customers, and our communities. Care is placing people at the centre of every decision - creating an environment where everyone feels valued, respected, and supported in their journey.

02
Competence

We strive to do everything with excellence and professionalism. Competence means delivering expertise, quality, and operational consistency - upholding the highest standards in everything we do, every single day.

03
Belonging

We create an environment where everyone feels valued and respected. Belonging means building a business that reflects and serves Mauritius - where every individual contributes to a shared mission built on pride and purpose.
