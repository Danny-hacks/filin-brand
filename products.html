Lubricants

Protection for Engines, Equipment and Fleets

Fillin lubricants support every engine that keeps Mauritius moving — from the family saloon on the school run and the delivery van crossing the island to the workshop in Quatre Bornes and the construction site running through midday heat.

The right lubricant is not just a maintenance decision — it is about keeping a vehicle on the road, a workshop productive, or a fleet on schedule. Fillin lubricants are matched to the conditions of island driving: humidity, heat, salt air and roads that demand consistent engine protection.

Whether you are a car owner, a workshop manager or a fleet operator, Fillin can help you select and supply the correct grade — reducing wear, extending service life and cutting unnecessary downtime.

Automotive

Engine protection for private cars, motorbikes and light commercials — from the morning commute to the weekend drive.

Commercial Fleet

High-mileage performance for transport, delivery and business vehicles covering every district of the island.

Industrial

Specialist grades for construction machinery, workshops and heavy-use equipment operating in Mauritius's climate.

Product Guidance

Help matching the right grade and supply route to your vehicle, fleet or operation — just ask the team.
