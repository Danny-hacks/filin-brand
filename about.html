Company Overview

More Than Just Fuel

Engen Petroleum (Mauritius) Limited is a leading energy company in Mauritius, delivering high-quality fuels, lubricants, and convenience retail services across the island. Following its acquisition by Bhunjun Group through Beta Oil Terminal Ltd, the company is now fully Mauritian-owned.

Our stations are more than refuelling stops. They are part of the daily rhythm of Mauritian life - designed around real people and real needs: the family on a weekend drive, the worker stopping in before a long shift, the commuter who counts on us every morning. Fuel moves vehicles, but care moves people.

Our Story

Fuel Moves Vehicles. Care Moves People.

Engen Mauritius was built on a belief that goes beyond fuel: that the way you are treated when you stop matters just as much as the quality of what goes in your tank.

Imagine a place where encounters and exchanges move you. Not just fuel for your tank - but a moment of care in your day. Designed around real lives: women who want to feel safe, elders who deserve patience, workers who deserve respect. At Engen, when you stop to refuel your car, you also refuel yourself. Health, Safety, Environment & Quality

HSEQ - Our Licence to Operate

HSEQ is our licence to operate, guiding every decision and action across our business. It is not a set of compliance checkboxes - it is the foundation of every operational decision, every station design, and every team member's daily practice. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of health, safety, environmental responsibility, and quality throughout all our operations.

Health & Safety

We prioritise the wellbeing of our employees, customers, and communities through continuous training, rigorous risk assessments, and robust safety systems - ensuring an incident-free environment across all operations.

Environmental Responsibility

We are committed to the responsible handling, storage, and distribution of petroleum products, taking every necessary measure to minimise our environmental impact and protect the natural heritage of Mauritius.

Quality Assurance

We operate to internationally recognised standards, applying rigorous quality control throughout our supply chain to ensure that every product and service we deliver is consistent, reliable, and trustworthy.

Continuous Improvement

We continuously review our systems, processes, and capabilities to raise the bar on safety, environmental performance, and quality - embedding a culture where every team member is empowered to improve.

Our HSEQ Commitment

  • HSEQ is our licence to operate
  • Zero harm to people, assets, and the environment
  • Full compliance with regulatory and international standards
  • Consistent delivery of high-quality products and services
