Engen Mauritius

Company Overview

More Than Just Fuel

Engen Petroleum (Mauritius) Limited is a leading energy company in Mauritius, delivering high-quality fuels, lubricants, and convenience retail services across the island. Following its acquisition by Bhunjun Group through Beta Oil Terminal Ltd, the company is now fully Mauritian-owned.

Our stations are more than refuelling stops. They are part of the daily rhythm of Mauritian life — designed around real people and real needs: the family on a weekend drive, the worker stopping in before a long shift, the commuter who counts on us every morning. Fuel moves vehicles, but care moves people.

Our Story

Fuel Moves Vehicles. Care Moves People.

Engen Mauritius was built on a belief that goes beyond fuel: that the way you are treated when you stop matters just as much as the quality of what goes in your tank.

Imagine a place where encounters and exchanges move you. Not just fuel for your tank — but a moment of care in your day. Designed around real lives: women who want to feel safe, elders who deserve patience, workers who deserve respect. At Engen, when you stop to refuel your car, you also refuel yourself. Engen Mauritius — Brand Manifesto

Our Vision

To be the most trusted, human-centred fuel network in Mauritius — where every stop is a moment of care, quality, and belonging for every customer we serve.

Our Mission

To deliver quality fuels, lubricants, and convenience retail through a safe, well-run network of stations — staffed by people who genuinely care about the customers and communities around them.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Every decision, every station, every interaction — guided by five values that define who we are and how we operate across Mauritius.

Responsibility

We own our actions and their impact — on customers, communities, and the environment at every station in our network.

Care

Every person who stops at an Engen station matters. Genuine concern for customer well-being drives every interaction.

Integrity

We operate with transparency — in how we fuel, how we price, and how we communicate with every customer and stakeholder.

Respect

Belonging

Safety & Sustainability

HSEQ at the Core

Health, Safety, Environment, and Quality (HSEQ) are fundamental to how we operate. HSEQ is our licence to operate, guiding every decision and action across our business — not compliance checkboxes, but the foundation of every operational decision, every station design, and every team member's daily practice.

Health & Safety

Rigorous safety protocols at every station, continuous staff training, and clear emergency procedures protecting customers and employees at all times.

Environmental Commitment

Responsible fuel handling, spill prevention, and ongoing investment in environmentally sound station infrastructure across the network.

Quality Assurance

Every litre of fuel dispensed meets strict international quality standards — consistent, tested, and traceable from terminal to pump.

Sustainability & CSR

Sustainability and corporate social responsibility are rooted in a simple belief: we are part of Mauritius, and we have a responsibility to contribute positively to its future — through community development and social support programmes, partnerships with local stakeholders, and initiatives that promote wellbeing, safety, and inclusion.

Our HSEQ Commitment

  • Zero harm to people, assets, and the environment
  • Full compliance with regulatory and international standards
  • Consistent delivery of high-quality products and services
  • To operate responsibly and sustainably for future generations
  • To support and uplift Mauritian communities
